# Commercial Licensing

Commercial use of **Destination Paper Collage Poster** requires prior written authorization.

## Commercial use examples

A commercial license is required for uses including:

- online or physical poster sales
- digital print products
- paid client design work
- brand campaigns and advertising
- commercial social-media campaigns
- merchandise
- paid templates or design packs
- paid AI workflows or creative services
- integration into a commercial product, platform, or subscription
- commercial distribution of outputs materially generated using this Skill

## License terms

Commercial licenses may be negotiated according to:

- duration
- exclusivity
- territory
- sales / distribution scale
- media type
- product category
- whether the Skill itself is distributed or only used internally
- whether generated outputs are sold directly

Possible structures include:

- fixed-term non-exclusive license
- fixed-term exclusive license
- perpetual non-exclusive license
- perpetual exclusive license
- project-specific commercial license

## How to request a license

Contact the repository owner through the contact method listed on the GitHub profile or open a licensing inquiry in the repository.

Please include:

- intended commercial use
- product or service type
- target market / territory
- expected duration
- whether exclusivity is required
- whether customers receive only final outputs or also receive the Skill / prompt system

No commercial rights are granted until written authorization is issued.
